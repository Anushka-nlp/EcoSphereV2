EchoSphere

Smart AI-Powered College Announcement Management System

Version: 1.0

Document Type: Software Requirements Specification (SRS)


---

1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

EchoSphere is a centralized college announcement platform designed to replace traditional notice boards and scattered communication methods.

The application enables students to instantly access official announcements through a mobile or desktop application while providing faculty members and administrators with an organized system for creating, approving, scheduling, managing, and broadcasting announcements.

Unlike a traditional ERP system, EchoSphere focuses exclusively on announcement management and AI-assisted communication.


---

1.2 Vision

Create a modern announcement ecosystem where:

Students never miss important announcements.

Teachers can publish announcements efficiently.

HoDs can manage departmental announcements.

Administrators can oversee the entire communication process.

AI assists users in creating, categorizing, validating, and delivering announcements.

College-wide announcements reach students through multiple communication channels.



---

1.3 Objectives

The primary objectives of EchoSphere are:

Eliminate dependency on physical notice boards.

Deliver announcements instantly.

Reduce communication delays.

Ensure announcements are approved before publication.

Prevent misinformation.

Prioritize emergency announcements.

Enable speaker-based announcements.

Provide an AI assistant for students.

Improve announcement quality using AI.



---

1.4 Scope

The system will support:

Android Application

Windows Desktop Application

(Future) iOS Application

Web-based Admin Panel (optional future enhancement)

3.1 Overview

The Announcement Management System is the primary feature of EchoSphere.

It provides a secure workflow for creating, reviewing, approving, scheduling, publishing, and distributing announcements across the college.

The system ensures that announcements reach the appropriate audience while enforcing institutional approval policies based on user roles.

3.11 Speaker Queue System

The speaker system includes a scheduling queue.

Rules:

Only speaker announcements enter the queue.

If multiple announcements are scheduled at the same time, they are placed in sequence.

A minimum two-minute gap is maintained between consecutive speaker announcements.

Higher-authority users (HoD and above, according to the original notes) can reorder or edit the queue when necessary. 



---

3.12 Editing Rules

Announcements may be edited only until 5–10 minutes before the scheduled publication or announcement time, as specified in the handwritten notes.

After this cutoff:

Editing is disabled.

The creator may delete the announcement instead (subject to role permissions).

A new announcement must be created if changes are required.

5. Artificial Intelligence System

5.1 Overview

EchoSphere includes an integrated AI system designed to enhance the user experience rather than replace existing application functionality.

The AI serves as an intelligent assistant for students, faculty, and administrators by helping users navigate the application, create professional announcements, validate content, answer institution-related questions, and improve communication efficiency.

The AI must not function as an unrestricted chatbot. It is limited to the institution's ecosystem and the application's features.

5.2 Objectives

The AI system aims to:

Simplify announcement creation.

Reduce human errors.

Help students find information quickly.

Improve announcement quality.

Automate repetitive tasks.

Provide voice interaction.

Recommend categories and priorities.

Detect duplicate announcements.

Assist users in navigating the application.

12.18 Disaster Recovery

In the event of failure:

1. Detect failure.


2. Notify administrators.


3. Restore the latest valid backup.


4. Restart services.


5. Verify data integrity.


6. Resume normal operations.



Documented recovery procedures should be maintained and tested periodically.
